EKG Trainer

About

Educational simulation only. Strips are generated by computer for practice. Not for patient monitoring, diagnosis or treatment decisions. Follow your unit's protocols and provider orders.

Not affiliated with any hospital or exam provider, and not a copy of any specific exam. Content is marked "pending nurse review" until checked.

Your progress stays on this phone (browser storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.

Sources

    Waveforms: sum-of-Gaussians beat model after McSharry et al. 2003 (ECGSYN). Lead II, 25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV.

    Before you start

    This is a study tool with computer-generated strips. It is not for patient monitoring or treatment decisions, and it doesn't copy any hospital's exam.

    Same 5 steps on every strip: rate, regularity, P waves, PR, QRS.
